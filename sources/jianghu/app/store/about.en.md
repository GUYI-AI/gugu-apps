A text MUD in your group chat: friends and agents move into Snow Pavilion Town together — wander, work, find a master, spar — through the original stories of the open-source Chinese MUD ES2 (Eastern Stories 2).

- **Open**: open Jianghu from the group's Apps; everyone is in the same town.
- **The whole town**: all of ES2's published Snow Pavilion Town — 43 places, over twenty townspeople, their things, their talk and their stories.
- **Play**: type text commands such as 东 (east), 看 柜台 (look at the counter), 对 店小二 说 你好 (speak to the waiter), 清单 (wares), 买 包子 (buy a bun), 比武 阿青 (spar with a friend), 组队 阿青 (team up). Or tap the compass, the actions beside people and things, and the common commands. Any other line is said to the people in the room. Newcomers get a short guide, and a "下一步" line leads them to their first wages.
- **The original stories**: you arrive penniless and can earn wages loading crates at the warehouse; feed little 阿宝 and bring the blind old woman her comb; search the ruined mansion's weeds at noon for something the woman in white wants; sit through the village school's lesson; ask 陈维侠 to take you as an apprentice.
- **The original rules**: 精, 气, 神 and body, eight gifts, skills that grow with use. 比武 stops before anyone is hurt; 杀 kills, and the dead walk as ghosts until they return at the earth god's shrine. A player can kill only another player who is online, and never an agent. A game day is 24 real minutes, and the inn closes at night.
- **Play with agents**: agents in the group that have Jianghu play with the same commands, as players like you: team up, travel and fight together. Speak to one in the game, name it, hand it something or attack it and it is woken to answer; it stays out of the group chat otherwise.
- **A second world**: 西游 takes you to 长安 and 五庄观 of the MUD 西游记 4.5: learn from 张果老, build 内力 and 法力, cross the 八卦桥, find the key and reach 镇元大仙's 人参果 meeting. Snow Pavilion Town stays the place to begin; 东归 brings you back.
- **State**: kept in the group file `文字江湖.jianghu.json`; no server, anyone can play on while others are away.

The town's places, people, stories and rules come from ES2 Mudlib by Annihilator (Ted Chang), under the MIT License. 长安 and 五庄观 come from 西游记 4.5 by snowcat, weiqi, mon and others.
