A text MUD in your group chat: friends and agents set out from 长安 on the road west together — find a master, train, clear each obstacle — through the original stories of the old Chinese MUD 西游记 4.5.

- **Open**: open Journey West from the group's Apps; everyone is in the same world.
- **Play**: type text commands such as 东 (east), 看 店小二 (look at the waiter), 对 店小二 说 你好 (speak to the waiter), 清单 (wares), 买 花生豆 (buy peanuts), 比武 阿青 (spar with a friend), 组队 阿青 (team up). Or tap the compass, the actions beside people and things, and the common commands. Any other line is said to the people in the room.
- **Pixel look**: drawn by hand in pixels: ten hearts, bolts, drumsticks and drops show 气血, 精神, food and water; the compass is a fixed three by three, with the ways you cannot go greyed out (up, down, in and out are typed). A new character opens on one title screen, then stands in 长安.
- **Journey to the West**: new characters start in 长安 of the MUD 西游记 4.5: learn from 张果老, build 内力 and 法力, and take the road west obstacle by obstacle: 五庄观's 人参果, rescuing the princess at 宝象国, 金角 and 银角 of 平顶山 with their magic gourd and vase, the drowned king of 乌鸡国, the three 大仙 of 车迟国, and 金鳞怪 under the ice of 通天河. The stories are the original's; a team shares each deed, so a veteran can bring a newcomer through 宝象国.
- **The rules**: 气血, 精神, 内力, 法力 and 道行, skills that grow with use. 比武 stops before anyone is hurt; 杀 kills, and the dead walk as ghosts until they return at 长安's city-god temple. A player can kill only another player who is online, and never an agent. A game day is 24 real minutes.
- **Play with agents**: agents in the group that have Journey West play with the same commands, as players like you: team up, travel and fight together. Speak to one in the game, name it, hand it something or attack it and it is woken to answer; it stays out of the group chat otherwise.
- **State**: kept in the group file `西游.jianghu.json`; no server, anyone can play on while others are away.

长安 and the road west as far as 通天河 come from 西游记 4.5 by snowcat, weiqi, mon and others. The pixel font is BoutiqueBitmap9x9 by Luke Liu (OFL).
