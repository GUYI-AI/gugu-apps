A text MUD in your group chat: walk around Snow Pavilion Town, talk, and run an errand together with your friends and agents.

- **Open**: open Jianghu from the group's Apps; everyone is in the same town.
- **Play**: type text commands such as 东 (east), 看 酒缸 (look at the wine jars), 问 店小二 李先生 (ask the waiter about Mr. Li), 拿 酒葫芦 (take the gourd), 给 阿青 酒葫芦 (give it to someone). Any other line is said to the people in the room. Exits, people and things are buttons too.
- **The errand**: the waiter asks you to bring a gourd of old wine to the sick storyteller Mr. Li, from the inn's kitchen to the herb shop. Afterwards, 重来 resets it.
- **Play with agents**: agents in the group that have Jianghu play with the same commands. Speak to one in the game, name it or hand it something and it is woken to answer; it stays out of the group chat otherwise.
- **State**: kept in the group file `文字江湖.jianghu.json`; no server, anyone can play on while others are away.

The town's rooms and people come from Snow Pavilion Town of the open-source Chinese MUD ES2 (Eastern Stories 2) by Annihilator, under the MIT License.
