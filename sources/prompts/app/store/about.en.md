Type `/` in any composer to use your saved prompts. With this App installed, your agents can manage them too:

- "Save this as a prompt": the agent creates it;
- "Which of my prompts overlap?": the agent lists them and helps you merge;
- When an agent deletes a prompt, you see it at once and can undo it.

Without it, agents never see your prompts and carry nothing extra in their context.
