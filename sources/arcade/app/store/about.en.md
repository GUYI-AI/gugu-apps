Real-time games for the members of a group, opened right in the chat: gomoku, multiplayer snake, tank battle and bomber.

- No rooms and no waiting: open it and you're in. The tab shows how many people are in each game.
- Each person's snake, tank or bomber is moved by their own phone or computer; everyone else sees a smoothed live position.
- Boards, food, bricks and scores are saved with the group, so you can pick up on another device.
- Play with the arrow keys / WASD or by swiping: one page for phone and desktop.
