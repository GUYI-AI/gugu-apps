# Screenshot source

`1-snake.png`, `2-tanks.png`, `3-bomber.png` and `4-gomoku.png` are screen captures of 联机小游戏 0.1.0 running in the Gugu Android app on an Android emulator (Pixel 6 profile, Android 14), captured on November 7, 2026 (UTC). Each is cropped to the App's page; nothing was redrawn.

Four example accounts (阿青, 小林, 周周, 老陈) were in the same group, three on desktop clients and one on the emulator, all playing at once. Their moves were made by a test script that pressed the same keys and clicked the same places a person would; the scores and positions are what the App showed.
