# Changelog

## 0.1.2

English: the arcade's tabs, buttons and score lines follow the client's language, Chinese for a Chinese client and English for any other.

## 0.1.1

An arcade look (pixel fonts, pixel-edged buttons, a night screen for the action games, a wooden 五子棋 board), and 五子棋 seats are no longer locked: a seated player can stand up, and a seat whose player left can be taken.

五子棋 also draws its board again after a resize; resizing a window or turning a phone left it blank until the next move.

## 0.1.0

First version: 五子棋, 多人贪吃蛇, 坦克大战 and 炸弹人 on one group file, real-time over presence.
