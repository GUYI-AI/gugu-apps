Read and highlight in WeRead; ask about it in gugu.

Once connected, the side pane shows the book you are reading (chapter, progress, time spent) and the lines you just highlighted. Pick a highlight and choose "Explain", "Another angle" or "Make connections": the highlighted text, where it is from and your own thought go into the message box, and you press Send. For a hard passage in maths or computing, highlight it in WeRead first, then ask here. "Notes by book" lists every highlight and thought in a book by chapter, and one click asks the agent to turn them into reading notes by theme, saved to the group folder when you are in a group.

Agents can read on their own too: ask "what does the line I just highlighted mean", "what am I reading" or "organise my notes on this book", and they fetch your newest highlights, thoughts and progress.

It uses WeRead's official open API (the WeRead Skill): get an API key (it starts with wrk-) in the WeRead app under Me › Settings › WeRead Skill, or at weread.qq.com/r/weread-skills, and paste it once. The API does not give the books' text; agents see your highlights, thoughts, chapters and progress. Runs on computers only.
