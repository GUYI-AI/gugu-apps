A board for a group chat: every job you hand out is a card, in five columns by whose move it is — to do, in progress, your turn, next Beta, delivered — plus on hold.

Cards that need your call sit under "your turn": the agent writes the options and its recommendation; one click (or "take all recommendations") tells the responsible agent in your name and moves the card back to in progress. Drafts get "OK" or "send back" with one line why.

Open a card for its milestone timeline, to-dos, the calls you made and comments; "go chat" jumps to the topic or session where the work happens. Conversations and code stay where gugu already keeps them — the board is for handing out work, deciding and seeing progress.

Agents read and write cards by ID: `task` reads a whole card with the board's rules and memory; `log`, `todo` and `comment` write to it; `cards` and `put` list, create and move cards.
