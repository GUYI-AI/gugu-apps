Change slides and spreadsheets with an agent, in a chat. The .pptx and .xlsx files in a chat's folder open with a click; pick or box the shapes to change on a slide, or box cells in a sheet, and they go into the composer to hand to an agent along with what you say. What the agent changes is the same file's next version; older versions stay in its history. Gugu Office's own page lists the decks and workbooks you opened lately, opens the chat's files in one click, and makes a blank deck or sheet. On a phone you can view and select, too.
