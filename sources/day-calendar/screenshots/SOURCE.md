# Market media sources

`side.png`, `side-dark.png`, `week.png` and `week-dark.png` are captures of Calendar 0.5.4 in gugu's desktop client on Linux, 1280 × 900, light and dark (host and App both in the dark theme). They were taken in a test account during the 0.5.x native run. The interface was not redrawn.

- `side.png` / `side-dark.png`: a task where the person asks an agent to put a viewing on tomorrow's calendar and the agent records it with `record`; the calendar is open in the task's side panel on today.
- `week.png` / `week-dark.png`: the calendar as a full tab in the week view, opening at 07:30. The entries were recorded for the example by the person and by an agent.

`promo.png` is `scripts/promo/day-calendar.html` rendered at 1600 × 900, device scale 1, with `side.png` as its product image. `icon.png` is unchanged.
