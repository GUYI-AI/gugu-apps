A calendar on a time axis. In a task's side panel it is one day: this week on a strip at the top, then the day from morning to night. Opened full page it is a week, with a small month calendar on the left.

## On the page

- Each item sits at its hour: to-dos are grey with an ink edge, crossed out once done; memories are blue. Items an agent wrote carry a ⚡. Items at the same time sit side by side.
- To-dos without a time go in the "All day" row at the top.
- A line and a time label on the left mark now.
- Click an empty hour (or press and drag down for a span), write a line, choose to-do or memory, and press Enter; the box at the bottom adds an all-day to-do.
- Click an item for its card: details, tags, when it was written, back to the task that wrote it, delete. Tick a to-do's circle to finish it.
- Side panel: click a day on the week strip to see it; expand the whole month at the top right. Full page: switch Day / Week, use the arrows to move a week (or a day), and Today to come back.

## Data

Everything stays on this computer, in this App's own data, and is deleted with the App. Your changes on the page and what an agent records go the same way, so neither overwrites the other; an agent's entry shows on an open page within seconds.

Tell an agent in a task "note this down" or "remind me tomorrow afternoon", and it writes it here. Only you, on the page, tick items off and delete them.
