Read alone, or make a chat a reading group. A paper's PDF stays in a project folder on this computer (alone) or in a chat's folder (together); highlights, discussions and study cards are kept next to it in `<paper name>.paper.json`. People and agents take part as themselves, and every citation opens the paper at that spot.

- **Alone**: open the App's own page (its icon in the left bar), choose a project on this computer under Storage, and import by arXiv ID, DOI or link, or upload a PDF; it opens once imported. "Ask an agent" about a passage picks one of this computer's tasks (the same project's first) and puts the passage, its page and path in that task's composer for you to send; or start a new task in the project. The sources in the agent's answer link to this PDF, and a click opens it there. Projects on this computer are not readable on a phone; use a chat's reading group there.

- **Read**: open a PDF in a chat's folder. Select text to highlight, or box a figure or formula; every highlight has its own thread. The top bar shows who is reading which page; click a face to follow them.
- **What the chat said**: each highlight shows how often the chat talked about it; expand it for those messages, click to jump to the chat.
- **Ask**: select a passage and press "Ask an agent"; it goes into the chat's composer as a pill to @ an agent with. The agent reads the whole paper and finds evidence, and every source in its answer is a pill.
- **Learn**: the study cards an agent writes each cite the paper. Everyone reviews with spaced repetition; misses and mastery are kept, on desktop and phone alike.
- **Import**: in the chat's bookshelf in the side panel, paste an arXiv ID, DOI or link, or upload a PDF.

Agents can read the whole paper, see highlights and discussions, find evidence, cite the original, comment, write study cards and import papers; the App has taught them how a reading group works.

PDFs are rendered with [PDF.js](https://github.com/mozilla/pdf.js) (Apache-2.0); its license ships with the App.
