# Market media sources

`1.jpg`–`3.jpg` show the Paper Club 0.3.8 reader (`reader.html`, the source merged in #165) rendered in Chromium with gugu's own App stylesheet and elements (`ui.css` / `ui.js` of the gugu 0.2.58 App runtime), light theme, 1200 × 750 CSS pixels at 1.2× (1440 × 900). The interface was not redrawn. The App ran against a stand-in host holding example data: a three-page excerpt typeset from the abstract, introduction and ablation of "Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models" (Wei et al., 2022; the figure's bars illustrate its reported trend), five highlights, three comments and four chat messages written for the example by two people and one agent. No model output is claimed.

1. A highlight selected: its thread with a member's and an agent's comment, a one-line reply beside "Ask an agent".
2. A sentence selected in the PDF text layer: highlight colours, comment, ask an agent.
3. The Discussed filter, with the chat messages citing a passage unfolded.

`promo.png` is `scripts/promo/paper-club.html` rendered at 1600 × 900, device scale 1, with `1.jpg` as its product image. `icon.png` is unchanged.
