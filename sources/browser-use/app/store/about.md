Lets an agent browse, click, fill in and take screenshots in its own session's Gugu browser tab. It uses that tab's browser profile and sign-ins; no separate browser is started.

Click the App to open the settings and status page Gugu provides: choose the start page for new tabs, control whether agents may use it, see the current page, and stop or resume the agent. Browser profiles and imported sign-ins stay in Gugu's Settings → Browser.

When you click into the page the agent is working on, or press Stop, the agent pauses; it resumes only when you press "Let the agent continue". Uninstall or switch off this App and agents can no longer call its tools; browsing by hand works as before.

Gugu operates the pages itself; the tools come from Playwright MCP, licensed under Apache-2.0.
