Gugu Assistant: the panel beside your private chat with Gugu.

- Activity: the morning and evening briefs, the diary and today's plan Gugu writes, by day.
- Tasks: what it has done and is doing, each opening its conversation; turn scheduled tasks on or off, or delete them.
- Memory: what it keeps about you, the files you gave it, and your voice notes' summaries and transcripts; upload, organise and delete files.
- Profile: its name, look and personality, its projects, devices and connected apps.

Buttons such as "Ask Gugu" or "Edit in chat" only put a sentence into that chat's composer; you decide whether to send it.
